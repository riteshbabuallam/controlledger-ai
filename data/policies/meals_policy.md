# Meals and Hospitality Policy

Policy: Meals and Hospitality Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Jurisdiction: Global

## [MEALS-2.1] Receipt evidence
An itemized receipt is required for meal claims above INR 500. The receipt total must agree with the claimed amount within INR 1.

## [MEALS-4.2] Daily meal allowance
The reimbursable meal allowance is INR 1,500 per employee per day. Amounts above the allowance require human review and must not be automatically approved.

## [MEALS-5.1] Business purpose
Meals must have a documented business or approved travel purpose. Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

## [MEALS-6.4] Split transactions
Multiple same-day claims at the same merchant must be considered together. Splitting a purchase to avoid a receipt or approval threshold is a policy violation.
