# Travel and Lodging Policy

Policy: Travel and Lodging Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Jurisdiction: Global

## [TRAVEL-2.1] Preapproval
All travel-related meals, lodging, and transport expenses require an approved trip record before the expense date. Missing or unapproved travel must be rejected or sent for human review when enterprise context is unavailable.

## [TRAVEL-3.2] Approved dates
Travel expenses are reimbursable only when the expense date falls from one day before the approved trip start through one day after the approved trip end.

## [TRAVEL-4.1] Hotel allowance
Hotel expenses must not exceed INR 9,000 per night. Higher amounts require documented executive exception and human review.

## [TRAVEL-5.3] Currency conversion
Foreign-currency claims must use the corporate exchange rate for the expense date. A variance greater than 3 percent requires human review.

## [TRAVEL-6.1] Weekend expenses
Weekend travel expenses require an active approved trip covering the expense date; otherwise they are not reimbursable.
