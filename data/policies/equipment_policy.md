# Equipment and Miscellaneous Expense Policy

Policy: Equipment and Miscellaneous Expense Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Jurisdiction: Global

## [EQUIP-2.2] Equipment allowance
Equipment purchases up to INR 25,000 may be reimbursed when supported by an itemized receipt and sufficient cost-center budget. Higher amounts require human review.

## [EQUIP-3.1] Eligible categories
Eligible categories are meals, hotel, transport, equipment, and miscellaneous business expenses. Unsupported or personal categories must not be automatically approved.

## [EQUIP-4.3] Duplicate claims
A receipt or materially identical purchase may be reimbursed only once. A confirmed duplicate is a definite violation and must be rejected.

## [EQUIP-5.2] Budget availability
A claim without sufficient available cost-center budget must be routed to human review.

## [EQUIP-7.1] Evidence retention
The policy version, evidence, control results, reviewer decision, and override reason must be retained in the Decision Passport.
